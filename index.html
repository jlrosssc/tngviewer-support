Before you contact us

  • Include the app version (Settings → About, or the App Store listing) and your iOS/iPadOS version.
  • Describe what you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Screenshots help a lot if the issue is visual.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use TNG Viewer?

TNG Viewer connects to a family tree website built with The Next Generation of Genealogy Sitebuilding (TNG). You'll need the web address of a TNG site and, if the site requires it, a username and password provided by the site's administrator.

The app can't connect to my TNG site. What should I check?

Verify the site address is entered exactly as it appears in your browser, confirm the site is reachable from your device's browser, and check that your login credentials are current. If the problem persists, email us with the site address (you can omit credentials) and we'll help troubleshoot.

I can't see photos or certain people in the tree.

TNG sites control visibility per user. If records or media appear on the website but not in the app, contact your site administrator to confirm your account's access rights, or email us and we'll help figure out what's going on.

How do I request a new feature?

Email us or open a GitHub issue describing what you'd like the app to do. We review every request.

What TNG Viewer does

TNG Viewer is a companion app for browsing a family tree hosted with The Next Generation of Genealogy Sitebuilding (TNG), letting you search people, view family details, and browse photos from your phone or tablet.

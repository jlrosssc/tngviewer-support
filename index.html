TNG Viewer Support

Help and support for the TNG Viewer iOS app

Get help

If you're running into a problem, have a question, or want to request a feature, please open a ticket on our GitHub issue tracker. We read every report and typically respond within a few days.

Open a support request

Before you file a request

  • Include the app version (Settings → About, or the App Store listing) and your iOS/iPadOS version.
  • Describe what you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Screenshots help a lot if the issue is visual.

What TNG Viewer does

TNG Viewer is a companion app for browsing a family tree hosted with The Next Generation of Genealogy Sitebuilding (TNG), letting you search people, view family details, and browse photos from your phone or tablet.

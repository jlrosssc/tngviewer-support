TNG Viewer Support

Help and support for the TNG Viewer iOS app

Who this app is for

TNG Viewer is intended for family members and guests who already have an approved login on our TNG genealogy site, rosssgenealogy.net. Signing in with your approved account unlocks the app's additional visual and mapping capabilities — family photos and documents, and interactive maps of the places in the family tree. If you don't have a login yet, email us to request one.

Contact us

If you're running into a problem, have a question, or want to request a feature, email us and we'll get back to you — no account required. We read every message and typically respond within a few days.

Getting connected

What do I need to use TNG Viewer?

TNG Viewer connects to a family tree website built with The Next Generation of Genealogy Sitebuilding (TNG). You'll need three things:

  • An approved TNG login — the same username and password you use on the website. For our family site, logins are issued by the administrator; email us to request one.
  • The web address of the TNG site. For our family tree, this is rosssgenealogy.net.
  • The tng_api.php script installed on that site. This is already in place on rosssgenealogy.net; the questions below about installing it only apply if you host your own TNG site.

How do I install the API script on my TNG site?

TNG Viewer talks to your site through a small script called tng_api.php. Upload it to your TNG root directory — the same folder that contains TNG's config.php — using FTP, SFTP, or your web host's file manager. No TNG configuration changes are needed. If you need a copy of the script, email us and we'll send it along with instructions.

How do I check that the script is installed correctly?

In any web browser, visit https://yoursite.com/your-tng-path/tng_api.php?action=ping. If you see a short response that includes "success": true, the script is installed and reachable. If you see a "not found" page instead, the script isn't in the right folder — it must sit next to config.php.

What do I enter on the app's setup screen?

For our family site, simply enter rosssgenealogy.net and tap Connect. In general, the setup screen takes the base URL of a TNG installation — the address of the page where TNG is hosted, without any .php filename. For example, if you view a tree at https://example.com/genealogy/index.php, enter https://example.com/genealogy. You can leave off https://; the app adds it automatically.

The app says "Cannot find host" or "Connection timed out."

Check the address for typos, and confirm the site opens in Safari on the same device. If the site works in Safari but not in the app, make sure you entered the base URL (no .php at the end) and that tng_api.php responds to the ping test above.

The app reports an SSL or HTTPS error.

iOS expects sites to use HTTPS. If your TNG site doesn't have an SSL certificate, the connection may fail or you may need to enter the address with http:// explicitly. The best fix is to add an SSL certificate to your site — most web hosts offer free certificates via Let's Encrypt. Email us if you're stuck and we'll help.

Do I have to sign in?

Yes — the app is intended for people who already have an approved TNG login. Sign in with the same username and password you use on the website. Signing in unlocks protected records, data for living persons, and the app's additional visual and mapping capabilities, subject to the access rights your site administrator has given your account. Your credentials are stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device. If you don't have a login, email us to request one.

How do I switch to a different TNG site?

Open the Settings tab in the app and choose Change Site URL, then enter the new site's address. To start completely fresh, Settings also offers Reset App, which clears the saved site and sign-in.

Frequently asked questions

I can't see photos or certain people in the tree.

TNG sites control visibility per user. If records or media appear on the website but not in the app, contact your site administrator to confirm your account's access rights, or email us and we'll help figure out what's going on.

How do I request a new feature?

Email us or open a GitHub issue describing what you'd like the app to do. We review every request.

What TNG Viewer does

TNG Viewer is a companion app for browsing a family tree hosted with The Next Generation of Genealogy Sitebuilding (TNG), letting you search people, view family details, and browse photos from your phone or tablet.
